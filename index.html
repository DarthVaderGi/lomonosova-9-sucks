Six moves. Agree or challenge.

The available records establish that ITMO University’s heritage campus at Lomonosova Street 9, Saint Petersburg partially collapsed in 2019 and is now caught in restoration talks and court appeals.

To verifyThe official cultural heritage status, legal protection boundaries, current permits, and court rulings on the project.

For residents and local heritage defenders (whose views we have not yet verified), preserving the authentic historical building intact is the main priority, whereas for the owner (ITMO University), students, and staff, guaranteed physical safety comes first.

To verifyThe real maximum capacity of the campus buildings, and documented opinions from students, staff, and local residents.

Although retaining the structure would preserve the original internal walls through adaptive reuse, we propose replacing the unsafe interior with a modern campus.

To verifyTechnical inspection reports on the wooden floors, load-bearing capacity, and the impossibility of safe restoration without full demolition.

This is likely to deliver a completely safe, modern educational building while keeping the historic street facade, provided that we mitigate temporary student displacement.

To verifyEngineering calculations for facade retention, and the university’s capacity for temporary relocation of classes.

Strongest case against us. Strict heritage protection law is a major constraint alongside ongoing court disputes.

Our first step. Commissioning an independent structural survey.

To verifyKGIOP heritage regulations, court rulings, and the legal requirements for demolishing emergency structures behind a protected facade.

Our website shows ITMO students, staff, and local citizens the structural inspection evidence and compares restoration with facade-preserved rebuilding.

To verifyWhat project data is published for the public, and whether visitors understand our risk argument and can challenge it.
